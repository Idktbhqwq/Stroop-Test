Stroop Color-Word Experiment

Your task is to identify the INK COLOR of each word, not the word itself.

For example, if RED appears in blue ink, choose BLUE.

You will first complete a short practice round, followed by the experimental round.

Respond as quickly and accurately as you can. Do not use the keyboard; tap or click one of the four color buttons.

Your trial-level results are stored only in this browser until you download the TXT. This first version does not send data to a server.

Practice

There will be 8 practice trials. Try to get comfortable with choosing the ink color.

Practice complete

Now you will complete the real experiment.

There will be 48 trials.

Remember: choose the ink color.
